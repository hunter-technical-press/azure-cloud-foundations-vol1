# Known limitations - first edition

- The first edition did not receive a separate independent-reader usability test before release. A fresh-account technical simulation and internal corrected-source QA were completed; independent-reader usability validation remains a maintenance item.
- Azure is a changing service. Portal labels, service behavior, licensing, prices, free-service eligibility, and regional/SKU availability may differ after the September 2026 validation window.
- Standard_B2ats_v2 in West US 2 is dated tested evidence only. Readers must verify their own eligible region/SKU and price before deployment.
- Cost Analysis and tag-based reporting can lag. Direct resource/tag verification is the immediate control in the relevant lab.
- Public-source brand screening is not formal trademark/legal clearance.
